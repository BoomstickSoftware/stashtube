# 2026-11-10: UX revamp, phases U1 to U5

The plan from [[2026-11-10-architecture-and-ux-review]], approved by the user. What exists now is
described in [[ui-system]]; this page is what changed.

## What changed

- **U1, system**: colour tokens for meaning (`danger`, `warn`, `private`, `ok`, `scrim`), two
  small text sizes, named z layers, shared heights (`--tabbar-h`, `--dock-h`, `--header-top`),
  motion tokens; `IconButton`, `UndoButton`; one `Sheet` with a `size`; the Pin to home form
  written once instead of twice. The font stack no longer names a face nothing loaded.
- **U2, touch feel**: a global press state and focus ring; the sheet rises, has a grabber and
  drags down to dismiss; Filters, Delete, Shortcuts and the card menu share its entrance, scroll
  lock and focus return; toasts stack (three) and clear the tab bar and the dock; the playlist
  delete and the cache clear lost their `window.confirm` (Undo toast, plain toast); 44 px
  targets in the player bar, the dock, Stations, Playlists, the filter dialog, Home's row
  controls and the bulk bar.
- **U3, fewer taps**: [[continue-strip-on-home]]; a card with a progress bar resumes on tap;
  stars in the card menu (rate from any list); the scene page's actions are the stars on one
  line and four equal buttons under a finger; playlists reorder on a phone (Move up and down
  are visible there) and removing an item has Undo; a long category list folds to eight with
  "+N more"; search says when nothing matches.
- **U4, motion and speed**: pages cross-fade (view transitions, 160 ms, none under reduced
  motion); Home's skeleton is a hero and rows; the phone header slides away on scroll down.
- **U5, delighters**: the heart, the O drop and the tapped star pop; the mini-player has a
  progress line, a swipe down to stop, and Stop offers Resume in a toast.

## How it was verified

`check.mjs` (web typecheck, lint at its baseline of 5, 10 web tests); neutral `phone-flow`
screenshots of the card menu, the scene page, Stations, Home, History and the dock;
`webkit-mini-check` (all three restore paths) with view transitions on.

## Left for later

- Search recents: not built. A list of recent searches on the first screen of search cuts
  against Discreet and Private; it needs the user's say.
- Pull-to-refresh on Home, "Play next", remembered Browse section and sort, shared-element
  (card to player) transitions, skeletons for History, Discover and Related.
- One `Segmented` control for Browse, History and Settings; migrating the remaining raw buttons
  to `Button` / `IconButton`; the "⋯" sheet for station cards and Home rows; the Stations
  start paths merged.
- Not checked on the real iPhone: the sheet drag, the header slide and the press states were
  seen only in headless Chrome and Playwright WebKit.

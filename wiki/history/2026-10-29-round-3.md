# 2026-10-29: Round 3

## What changed

- **Stations page**: each card cycles screenshots of what it plays (4 s, crossfade, still
  under reduced motion) and shows its count; quick starts are cards; **New station** opens an
  editor (`/stations/new`, `/stations/<id>/edit`) with the filter panel over a live grid.
- **Resume**: a playing station saves its spot every 15 s and on leaving (`station_resume`);
  cards offer "Resume …"; **Shuffle** starts fresh; **Unwatched** plays only never-watched
  videos.
- **Station player** gets the video page's seek bar (sprites, markers; Moments shade the
  planned windows), ±10 s, previous video, Mark, Favorite and a list panel; keys arrows ±5 s,
  j/l ±10 s, n/p, b, [ ], q. Arrow right had skipped to the next video until now.
  → [[stations]], [[seek-bar-markers]].
- **Categories**: pin a tag (Stash's tag favourite) from its tile or page → [[people-pages]].
- **Studios**: Networks / Grid / Table views, sort, Following, kept per browser.
- **Filters rethought** (d8ee9cb): the chip strip with popovers is gone; every list has a
  Filters button, sort, and the active filters as removable chips; one dialog (full-screen
  sheet on phones) with one search over performers, studios, tags and title words, then
  Watched, Rating, Quality, Length, followed performers, age, ethnicity, Show blocked.
- **Live counts** in the panel (b5d3459): `GET /api/scenes/facets` tallies the light fields
  of every video in the set in the BFF (~0.5–0.9 s cold for ~2,000 videos); "N videos
  match", per-choice counts, "12 here · 865 in all" in search. → [[browse-and-filters]].
- Browse nav shows how many filters "Add to filters" has stacked.

## How it was verified

Hand use on the phone; headless checks of the filter dialog and station player.

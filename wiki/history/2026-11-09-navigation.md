# Five-tab navigation

Initiative G of [[roadmap]], decision [[five-tab-navigation]]; current behaviour in
[[navigation]].

## What changed

- Tabs Home · Browse · Stations · Discover · You on phone and desktop, lit by section.
- Header: Search, one Privacy menu (Discreet, Private session, Lock now), Settings; the Discover,
  Stations and Vault icons and the two privacy toggles left it.
- `BrowseStrip` on Browse and the three directories; new `/you` page.
- Station chips removed from the filter bar, the video page's Related and Home rows; the
  Stations tab's Start from (last filtered list, drift from the last video) replaces them.
- Settings › Feed › Home rows.

## Verified

`node scripts/check.mjs`; phone screenshots of Browse, Performers, Stations (Start from), You,
the Privacy menu, History and Settings › Home rows, and the desktop header at 1440 px.

## Snags

The route tree (`routeTree.gen.ts`) is regenerated by the Vite plugin; `npx tsr generate` fails
here, so start Vite once after adding a route, before `check.mjs`.

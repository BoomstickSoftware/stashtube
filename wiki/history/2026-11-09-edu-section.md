# 2026-11-09: Edu section

## What changed

- `edu_tags` table (migration `0007`), `edu.ts`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/edu/tags[/:id]`.
- Exclusions carry the Edu categories; `curated()` holds them out of Home and Featured,
  `noEdu` out of stations, `relatedSide()` keeps related videos on one side; affinity skips
  Edu scenes. `edu=1` lists only Edu.
- Web: `/edu` page, desktop nav item and phone header icon, an Edu toggle on category pages,
  Settings → Library → Edu section (search to add, × to remove).
- Phone header gap 16 → 0 px below `sm`: seven 44 px icons left the Settings gear outside the
  440 px row (measured: it sat at 455–499 px); now the row ends at 424 px.

## How it was verified

- `node scripts/check.mjs`: typecheck, web tsc, lint at baseline, 121 server tests including new
  `sceneFilter` and `isBlocked` cases for the Edu hold, privacy check.
- `scripts/phone-flow.mjs` at 440×956 against production through Vite, `/api/edu/tags` stubbed:
  Home, `/edu` (with categories and empty), a category page, Settings; no horizontal overflow.
- Not run against Stash locally: there was no `.env` in the checkout, so the Edu criteria were
  first sent to Stash in production after deploy (they are inert until a category is added).

## Snags

- **The first deploy (`9c4a895`) did not hold Edu out.** Stash ignored the separate Edu tag
  exclusion, which went unnoticed until a category was added and its videos were still on
  Featured and Recently added ([[second-tags-exclusion-ignored]]). Fixed the same day by merging
  the Edu categories into the blocked tags' criterion; `deploy-check.mjs` gained an Edu check
  that failed on the broken build and must pass on the fix.

- The route tree is regenerated by Vite's router plugin; `tsr` is not installed, so a
  `vite build` (or `pnpm dev`) is what updates `routeTree.gen.ts` after adding a route.

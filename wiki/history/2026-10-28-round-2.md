# 2026-10-28: Round 2

## What changed

- **Delete videos through Whisparr**, from the multi-select bar or next to Favorite on a
  scene (7a6a524, ea392e9; 86013a3 leaves the page cleanly; c37022a finishes deletes whose
  files Whisparr already removed and waits out a busy Stash) → [[delete-via-whisparr]].
- **Scene page** shows the full description; path, format and links fold under the tags
  (8d25082) → [[scene-page]].
- The home carousel previews on a slight drag on touch (46addbe) → [[home-feed]].
- **Cleanup page** `/cleanup`: below 720p, never watched in 30+ days, rated ★2 or less, not on
  StashDB, possible duplicates (a208719) → [[cleanup-page]].
- **Stackable filters** kept per browser, "+" on performers, studio and tags, "Add to filters"
  on their pages, a "+ Filter" search (a208719) → [[browse-and-filters]].
- **People filter**: age at filming and ethnicity matched on one performer of the Settings
  gender, with defaults in Settings › Performer preferences (707da69).
- **Studio families**: the Studios page grouped under networks, "All of …" tiles, counts that
  include sub-studios (ca89fc6) → [[people-pages]].
- **Stations** `/station`: endless full-screen play from any list's filters or drifting from a
  scene, Full or Moments mode (21b4a01); **saved stations** with a Stations page and
  quick-starts (e2c4629) → [[stations]].
- "Surprise me" dropped: the carousel already does it.
